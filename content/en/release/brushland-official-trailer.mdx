## Tips for Judging the Footage Before You Commit

Trailers sell feeling. Before you decide whether Brushland belongs on your wishlist, run the footage through a few filters.

- **Watch the cursor, not the camera.** Camera moves are authored; cursor behavior reveals real tools.
- **Count the building variety.** Repeated assets in a short trailer can signal a narrow initial content set.
- **Look for simulation feedback.** Do buildings react to terrain? Do roads follow contours? Those details hint at depth.
- **Check the UI density.** A busy interface usually means management systems; a minimal one suggests creative sandbox play.
- **Note what is missing.** No date, no price, and no platform list beyond PC means the game is still early in its marketing cycle.
- **Read the tags, not the comments.** Tags come from the trailer itself; comment sections come from excitement.
