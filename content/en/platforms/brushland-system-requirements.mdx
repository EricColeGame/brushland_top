## Brushland System Requirements at a Glance

Brushland is a fairytale kingdom-builder in development at Transhuman Design, the studio behind Soldat and King Arthur's Gold. It is currently listed as "coming soon" on Steam, so the only official hardware guidance available today is the minimum spec block on [Brushland's official Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5163450/Brushland/). Here is that information consolidated into a single view:

| Platform | OS | Processor | Memory | Graphics | Storage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Windows | Windows 10 / 11 | Intel Core i3-8100 or AMD Ryzen 3 3200G | 4 GB RAM | DirectX 11-compatible GPU | 1 GB available space |
| macOS | macOS 12 Monterey or newer | Intel Core i3 (quad-core, 3.6 GHz) or Apple M1 | 4 GB RAM | Metal-capable Intel and AMD GPUs | 1 GB available space |
| Linux | Ubuntu 22.04 / 24.04 (64-bit) | Intel Core i3-8100 or AMD Ryzen 3 3200G | 4 GB RAM | Vulkan-compatible GPU with up-to-date drivers | 1 GB available space |

Three details jump out. First, memory tops out at 4 GB on every platform, which is low enough that almost any computer sold in the last several years clears the bar. Second, the install footprint is just 1 GB — this is not a title that will force you to uninstall three other games. Third, and most importantly, Transhuman Design has not published a recommended tier at all, so treat the minimums as a floor rather than a target.

## Windows, macOS, and Linux: The Full Breakdown

### Windows 10 and 11

| Component | Minimum | What it means in practice |
|---|---|---|
| OS | Windows 10 or 11 | Older Windows versions are not listed as supported |
| Processor | Intel Core i3-8100 / AMD Ryzen 3 3200G | Both are entry-level quad-core desktop chips from the 2017–2019 era |
| Memory | 4 GB RAM | Enough to launch, tight if you multitask heavily |
| Graphics | DirectX 11-compatible GPU | Includes most integrated graphics from the past decade |
| Storage | 1 GB available space | Trivial for any modern drive |

The two named CPUs are modest quad-core parts, not gaming flagships. The Core i3-8100 runs at 3.6 GHz with four cores and no hyperthreading, while the Ryzen 3 3200G pairs four cores with Vega 8 integrated graphics. If your PC was built or bought any time after roughly 2018, you are probably already at or above this level.

The graphics line is deliberately broad. "DirectX 11-compatible" sweeps in Intel UHD and Iris integrated graphics, AMD Vega and RDNA integrated chips, and essentially every discrete GPU released in the last ten-plus years. There is no VRAM figure attached, which usually signals a game that leans on stylized art rather than heavy texture work.

### macOS and Linux

| Component | macOS minimum | Linux minimum |
|---|---|---|
| OS | macOS 12 Monterey or newer | Ubuntu 22.04 / 24.04 (64-bit) |
| Processor | Intel Core i3 (quad-core, 3.6 GHz) or Apple M1 | Intel Core i3-8100 / AMD Ryzen 3 3200G |
| Memory | 4 GB RAM | 4 GB RAM |
| Graphics | Metal-capable Intel and AMD GPUs | Vulkan-compatible GPU with up-to-date drivers |
| Storage | 1 GB available space | 1 GB available space |

The Mac listing is notable because Apple M1 appears by name, which suggests native Apple Silicon support rather than a Rosetta translation layer. Intel Macs need a quad-core chip at roughly 3.6 GHz plus a Metal-capable GPU; since Metal has shipped on every Mac for years, that requirement mostly filters out very old hardware rather than recent machines.

On Linux, the deciding factor is not the CPU but the driver stack. Brushland asks for a Vulkan-compatible GPU with current drivers, and Ubuntu 22.04 and 24.04 are the tested distributions. Other modern distros will likely work, but they are not officially listed, so plan on verifying your Vulkan setup yourself.

The pattern here is simple: Brushland's demands sit near the bottom of what modern storefronts ask for. The caveat is the missing recommended tier. Minimum specs tell you the game will run, not that it will run smoothly at high resolutions. If you are on a 4K monitor or a high refresh-rate display, expect to tune settings — and wait for post-launch performance reports from players before assuming anything.

## Tips to Meet the Brushland System Requirements on a Budget

If your machine is borderline, these steps will help you get the most out of it — no upgrades required.

- **Identify your exact hardware first.** On Windows, open Task Manager and check the Performance tab; on macOS, use About This Mac; on Linux, run `lscpu` and `vulkaninfo`.
- **Update your graphics drivers.** On Linux especially, the Vulkan requirement lives or dies by your driver version. Mesa and proprietary drivers both need to be current.
- **Free up more than 1 GB.** The listed storage figure is the install size, not the working space. Leave a few gigabytes of headroom for saves, shader caches, and updates.
- **Close background apps on 4 GB systems.** A browser with twenty tabs will eat your RAM budget before the game even loads.
- **Check your OS version.** Windows 10/11, macOS 12 Monterey, and Ubuntu 22.04 or 24.04 are the listed baselines — an outdated OS is the easiest requirement to fail.
- **Don't upgrade hardware yet.** With no release date and no recommended specs published, buying parts now is premature. Wishlist the game and revisit once final specs and player reports appear.

One more practical note: specs published months before launch are frequently revised as optimization work finishes. Checking the Steam page closer to release is the only reliable way to know the final numbers.

## FAQ

**What are the minimum Brushland system requirements?**
On Windows you need Windows 10 or 11, an Intel Core i3-8100 or AMD Ryzen 3 3200G, 4 GB of RAM, a DirectX 11-compatible GPU, and 1 GB of free space. Linux asks for the same CPU and memory with a Vulkan-capable GPU on Ubuntu 22.04 or 24.04, and macOS needs Monterey or newer with an M1 or quad-core Intel chip.

**Can my Mac run Brushland?**
If it is an Apple Silicon Mac or an Intel model with a quad-core 3.6 GHz processor, runs macOS 12 Monterey or later, and has a Metal-capable GPU with 4 GB of RAM, it meets the published minimums. Very old Intel Macs stuck on older versions of macOS will not qualify.

**Is Brushland verified for Steam Deck?**
Not yet. Steam lists Deck compatibility as unknown, so there is no official rating. The low Brushland system requirements suggest it could run well on the hardware, but wait for Valve's verification before counting on it.

**Will the Brushland system requirements change before release?**
They might. The game is still marked "coming soon," and no recommended specs have been published. Developers often adjust both minimum and recommended tiers during final optimization, so confirm the numbers on the store page near launch.

For now, the takeaway is encouraging: this is one of the least demanding kingdom-builders on the horizon, and most computers from the past several years should clear the bar without breaking a sweat.
